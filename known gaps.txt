### GAP 2 — BHS Monitoring Daemon Runs On-Demand
**Status:** Minor operational gap

`monitor_bhs.py` is fully functional and runs correctly when called. 
It is not currently configured as a persistent background service. 
The Red-Line crisis protocol triggers correctly when BHS drops below 50 
— this has been drill-tested and verified with a simulated BHS=30 crisis.

**To make it persistent:** Configure monitor_bhs.py as a system service 
using systemd, supervisor, or a simple cron job. Estimated effort: 
15-30 minutes for a developer familiar with Linux services.

**Buyer impact:** Minimal — the monitoring works, it just requires a 
manual trigger or simple service configuration rather than running 
automatically in the background.
